Understanding the signals

The purpose of an intelligence briefing is not to accumulate more information. It is to distinguish consequential developments from noise, examine their evidence, and understand how separate systems interact.

Technology and human agency

Frontier artificial intelligence is changing how people research, build, and coordinate work. The central question is not simply what increasingly capable systems can do, but whether their deployment improves human judgment, security, and opportunity.

The household economy

Economic growth and household flourishing are related, but they are not identical. Oikonomia originally concerned the stewardship of the household. Eudaimonia asks a wider ethical question: what conditions help human beings live well?

Systems convergence

Artificial intelligence, energy, labour markets, and public institutions influence one another. A useful briefing makes those connections visible without mistaking forecasts for facts.
