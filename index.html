About Desmond

Desmond Wood is a former Buddhist monk, contemplative teacher, writer, and AI-native builder based in Carmel, Indiana. His work brings disciplined contemplative training into direct conversation with emerging technology: how to strengthen attention, exercise judgment, and expand what people can accomplish with AI.

Ordained in the Trúc Lâm Thiền tradition in 2019 and later fully ordained in the Theravāda tradition, he concluded monastic life in December 2024. His technical education includes Agents and Workflows through OpenAI Academy, AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations through Anthropic Education, Google Prompting Essentials, and IBM coursework in software, programming, and databases.

He puts that training into practice through Aware Presence, Nuevo Flow, and the interactive Reader Experience for IAM Intelligence (Intelligence Amplification Mastery). He explores how increasingly capable tools can serve clearer thinking, useful work, and greater economic agency. His approach joins hands-on building with the contemplative concern for how people live, work, and relate to one another.

The Framework

Measure what becomes possible.

An ongoing inquiry into whether AI progress translates into greater human capability. These three benchmarks guide the inquiry; the documented artifacts above are an initial record. Changes in income, skill, and economic agency remain outcomes to measure.

Own problems. Direct intelligence. Verify outcomes. Build relationships. Create economically valuable things.

Intelligence Organization

AI can help organize scattered knowledge, ideas, goals, priorities, and attention. The benchmark is how reliably that support becomes coherent plans, decisions, systems, and finished work. Guidance helps people put this capability into practice.

Income Generation

Can an agent help a person earn legitimate new income, from first dollars to repeatable monthly revenue? This remains a possibility to test and document, with outcomes that depend on the person, the work, and the market.

Highest-level benchmark

Economic Agency Amplification

How much does an agent expand the range, quality, and economic value of actions a person can successfully undertake? This overarching benchmark encompasses organization and income generation, and asks whether new capabilities hold up in practice.

Explore the operating model and outcome questions

An Essential Distinction

Traditional productivity

“How much faster can AI help a worker perform an existing task?”

Intelligence Amplification

“What valuable things can this person now accomplish that were previously outside their effective capability?”

A human-led operating model

Agency stays with the person.

The human directs intelligence and owns the outcome. The agent performs bounded digital work.

Human role

  • Choose the problem
  • Define intent and constraints
  • Direct intelligence
  • Verify outputs
  • Exercise judgment
  • Communicate with people
  • Build trust
  • Own the outcome

Agent role

  • Research
  • Analyze
  • Code and test
  • Draft
  • Transform data
  • Organize information
  • Generate options
  • Iterate
  • Execute bounded digital work

The real benchmark

Measure the change in human capability.

  • Did the person earn more?
  • Did valuable assets get created?
  • Did unfinished work become finished?
  • Did new customers appear?
  • Did effective skill surface expand?
  • Did dependence on low-leverage labor decrease?
  • Did one person become capable of operating like a much larger organization?

Capability backdrop

A rapid rise in capability.

From the first GPT in 2018 to today’s frontier models, AI has moved from language prediction toward increasingly capable reasoning, coding, and tool use. What matters next is what people can make possible with it.
